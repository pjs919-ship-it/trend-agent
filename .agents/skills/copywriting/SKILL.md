# Brand Copywriting, Messaging & Editing

Transforms brand strategy into sharp, provable human copy — matching the author's real voice and purging AI clichés in Korean and English.

## Before Writing: Load Context
1. Read `.agents/brand-context.md` (if present) and workspace constraints (`AGENTS.md` / `docs/POLICY.md` for legal/regulatory bans such as Cosmetic Act Art. 13 and UX writing standards).
2. Read the `Learnings` log so previously tested or rejected angles are not re-pitched.

## Step 1: Diagnose the Input Mode (Mandatory)
State the mode in 1 line before drafting or editing so you never steamroll good founder taste:
- **Mode A (Blank / Generic Slop)**: Build the argument from strategy & facts only. Zero invented stats, testimonials, or outcomes.
- **Mode B (Real Voice Present)**: Preserve the author's argument, stance, and vocabulary choices (`"ship"`, `"뽑다"`, colloquial cadence); only rescue buried leads, fix vagueness, and strip AI tells.
- **Mode C (Point Edit)**: Change **only** the requested headline/line plus the minimum around it to fit. Return everything else untouched; skip full sweeps.

## Step 2: Core Workflows

### Workflow 1 — Messaging Architecture (`What We Say`)
Build hierarchically from strategy down to touchpoints:
1. **Core Strategic Anchor (1 sentence)**: `"[Brand] helps [ICP] [concrete outcome] by [differentiator]."`
2. **Value Proposition (2–3 sentences)**: Who it's for + broken alternative + specific change + proof.
3. **Tagline Options (3–5)**: Mix *Functional*, *Sharp Editorial*, and *Emotional* (`3–7 words` or crisp Korean cadence); verify pairing `[Brand] · [Tagline]`.
4. **Hero & Section Copy (`Decompose & Lock`)**: Draft unit-by-unit (Headline → Subhead → Primary CTA → Proof Pillars → FAQ/Objections). Put punch in only 1–2 spots; keep body declarative and specific.

### Workflow 2 — Brand Voice Guide (`How We Sound`)
Define actionable voice rules, never generic adjectives alone:
- **Voice Essence (1 sentence)** + **4 Tone Axes** (`Casual ↔ Formal`, `Calm ↔ Urgent`, `Accessible ↔ Expert`, `Warm ↔ Sharp`) with exact *"Do / Don't"* and *"Before / After"* examples.
- **Lexicon**: 10 words/verbs we naturally use vs 10 words banned in this brand's domain.
- **Channel Adaptation Table**: Homepage Hero / Product UI & Empty States / Social Share (OG, SNS) / Email or Outreach / Support.

### Workflow 3 — Seven-Sweep Copy Editing (`Tighten Existing Drafts`)
For Mode A/B full edits, run focused sequential passes without changing core intent:
1. **Clarity**: Can a busy buyer picture it in 3 seconds? One idea per section.
2. **Voice & Tone**: Consistent personality; active verbs over passive nouns.
3. **So What?**: Every feature immediately answers *"what does this change for my Tuesday morning?"*
4. **Prove It & Claims Unlock Rule**: Unsupported claims must NOT be written or silently dropped — offer the closest supportable alternative and flag `[NEED: exact evidence that unlocks this claim]`.
5. **Specificity**: Concrete numbers, time saved, real scenario over *"workflow optimization"*.
6. **Strip AI Tells**: Check every pattern in [references/ai-tells-and-korean-polish.md](./references/ai-tells-and-korean-polish.md).
7. **Cut & Flow**: Read aloud; run the **Leave-It-Alone Test** (revert any edit you cannot prove is better).

## Mandatory Three-Question Line Gate
Every headline, value claim, or bullet must clear at least 2 (ideally all 3):
1. **Can the reader visualize a concrete scene or result?**
2. **Is it falsifiable (provably true or false vs puffery)?**
3. **Does it fail if pasted onto a competitor's homepage (Swap Test)?**
